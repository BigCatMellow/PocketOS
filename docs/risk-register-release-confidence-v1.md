# PocketOS Release Confidence V1 — Risk Register

| ID | Risk | Class | Severity | Owner | Mitigation | Trigger / evidence | Rollback or contingency | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| RCV1-R01 | Published/package behavior drifts behind the source tree used to justify it | release integrity | HIGH | orchestration operator | exact candidate freeze; package/source identity checks; separate build from publish; post-publish verification | package SHA/source revision mismatch, stale release metadata, or artifact contents differ from reviewed candidate | stop publication, keep distribution paused, rebuild from reviewed candidate | OPEN |
| RCV1-R02 | Important reliability/review claims cannot be traced to primary evidence | evidence provenance | HIGH | orchestration operator | preserve raw device logs, exact review records, hashes, revision bindings, and human-readable summaries | only summary prose survives; reviewer/device evidence cannot be recovered | reproduce evidence on fresh candidate rather than relying on historical claim | OPEN |
| RCV1-R03 | ROM/import/install/uninstall behavior damages user data or silently makes unsafe platform/target assumptions | data integrity | HIGH | bounded implementer for affected surface; orchestration operator integrates | deterministic adversarial tests first; disposable/cloned card for hardware effects; rollback/recovery verification | destructive mutation, guessed target, malformed-data overwrite, unsafe extraction/collision, failed rollback | stop affected branch, restore disposable test state, repair and re-verify before continuation | OPEN |
| RCV1-R04 | Implementer effectively reviews its own consequential work | review independence | HIGH | orchestration operator | assign fresh independent reviewer with minimal sufficient context and exact revision binding | reviewer authored output, inherited author reasoning, or review not bound to candidate SHA | invalidate review, route to fresh reviewer | OPEN |
| RCV1-R05 | Stabilization expands into features/refactors and delays release-confidence proof | scope/WIP | MEDIUM | orchestration operator | active roadmap excludes major feature families; keep future ideas parked; detail only current phase/first wave | task does not directly improve release truth, evidence, safety proof, release gating, or later approved maintainability | park task and return to next eligible release-confidence item | OPEN |
| RCV1-R06 | Host tests pass while Miyoo/Onion behavior differs materially | hardware/environment | HIGH | device validation operator | identify claims that require real hardware; use repeatable runbook; retain raw evidence | device-only failure, memory/runtime anomaly, input/audio/storage/handoff mismatch | block affected claim/release gate; reproduce and route bounded repair | OPEN |
| RCV1-R07 | Version identity exists in multiple mutable places and diverges | configuration/release integrity | HIGH | version-identity task owner; orchestration operator integrates | establish one canonical identity source and generated/validated downstream representations | code/environment/package/release title or metadata disagree | fail release gate; reconcile to canonical owner and regenerate | OPEN |
| RCV1-R08 | Branch protection, CI, review requirements, or live repository state changes underneath a prepared task/review | execution integrity | MEDIUM | orchestration operator | recover live state before consequential merge/release; exact-head checks; invalidate stale evidence after material change | base/head/check/review status changed, stale contract, failed merge gate | refresh/re-bind task/review; use MAPS_L revalidation only when reviewed content is actually unchanged | OPEN |
